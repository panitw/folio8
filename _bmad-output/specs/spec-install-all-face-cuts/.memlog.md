- (note) Trigger: owner installed Sarabun from the Add font dialog and both B and I reported no face in the family; investigation confirmed the install is Regular-only by design, not a failed download.
- (note) Evidence: font-source.ts:196-198 regularFilename() narrows a parsed family to its weight-400 normal, and font-source.ts:449-453 refuses a family that has none; style is hardcoded 'Regular' at font-source.ts:533; one fetch per family.
- (note) Evidence: METADATA.pb parse already carries every static face with weight+style (font-source.ts:116-121, :138-187), so the upstream data needed for the other three cuts is already in hand at pick time.
- (note) Evidence: the engine consumes cuts live at render (render.go:1360-1382 chainFaceNames, :2091-2098 variant used only if it covers the rune), so declared cuts change painted glyphs today; absence emits TEXT_STYLE_FACE_UNDECLARED (diag.go:145) and paints the base face.
- (note) Evidence: shipped-face-cuts.ts:66-83 plus shippedFamilyEntry (:118-141) is the working multi-cut model, used for Roboto and the Noto faces; a web family needs to reach the same entry shape.
- (note) Evidence: the asymmetry is stated in the code itself - App.tsx:2530-2534 and component_commands.go:4412-4418 both record that the picked entry declares no cut while the fallback tail does.
- (decision) Scope is the four cuts only - Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic (OWNER). Chosen because docs/folio-format.md:240-275 closes the entry's variant set to bold/italic/boldItalic; any further weight has nowhere to be declared and would cost a format MAJOR.
- (decision) Both tiers gain cuts (OWNER): the 31 committed catalogue families as well as web-fetched ones, so a family behaves the same whichever tier it came from. Costs repo bytes and offline-cache slots, and retires the Regular-only assertion at font-catalogue.test.ts:461.
- (decision) Already-saved documents are completed AUTOMATICALLY in the background on open, including an opened template's embedded fonts, with progress reported in the status bar (OWNER). Rejected both leaving them alone and prompting the author.
- (decision) Spec slug is install-all-face-cuts, a new spec rather than an amendment to SPEC-fonts (OWNER). It closes SPEC-fonts' open question 'Do bold and italic get realized in this scope?' by reference.
- (capability) CAP-1: picking a family from the Add font dialog embeds every one of the four cuts the family publishes, and the resulting chain entry declares them, so the B and I controls paint rather than warn.
- (capability) CAP-2: a family that genuinely publishes fewer than four cuts installs what exists and the panel's absence sentence stays truthful for the rest - the warning becomes a statement about the family upstream, not about the installer.
- (capability) CAP-3: the committed catalogue tier carries its families' cuts too, so a family behaves identically whichever tier it came from and the offline author is not the one who loses bold.
- (capability) CAP-4: a document or template already saved with a Regular-only family completes itself in the background on open, reporting progress in the status bar, without blocking the open or the author's editing.
- (capability) CAP-5: the designer models a family as a set of faces rather than one face, so a store holding four faces of one family offers that family once and resolves each cut to the right face.
- (constraint) The .folio format does not change. Four assets per entry, declared through the existing closed bold/italic/boldItalic keys; no new key, no version bump beyond the 2.0 an asset entry already declares.
- (constraint) Every embedded face carries its own licence record - docs/folio-format.md:960-961 already requires this of a variant asset key, so four cuts means four records and roughly four copies of the licence text. The duplication is already the format's accepted position (:964-969) and is not reopened here.
- (constraint) No synthetic bold or oblique and no variable axes. A cut is a real static face fetched from upstream or it is absent, and absence keeps its existing TEXT_STYLE_FACE_UNDECLARED warning and base-face paint.
- (constraint) The upright Regular stays the base of the entry and stays required. A family with no weight-400 normal is still refused at font-source.ts:449-453; the other three cuts are additions to that base, never substitutes for it.
- (constraint) Background completion never leaves a document that will not render, and never blocks opening. Offline or upstream-unreachable, the document opens exactly as it does today, the status bar says the cuts could not be fetched, and the existing warning stands.
- (constraint) The offline release asset budget is a build gate (release-payload.ts:65-91, maximum 90, currently around 61) and must still pass. Adding cuts to 31 committed families cannot be allowed to red that gate silently.
- (constraint) Byte identity holds. A document whose faces are unchanged renders to the same bytes, and a no-op round trip still rewrites the file byte-for-byte.
- (constraint) Each cut is fetched and verified on its own terms: the variable-font refusal at font-source.ts:513-515 and the ttf/otf media-type check at :206-211 apply per face, so one bad cut is refused without poisoning the family.
- (assumption) Assumed 'all the font faces' means the four cuts the typography panel can ask for, because that is what the owner's B/I controls request and what the format can declare; confirmed by the owner at breakdown.
- (assumption) Assumed background completion marks the document changed and waits for the author's save rather than writing the file itself - the designer has no other path that rewrites a file the author did not save. Listed as an open question because it is a behaviour the owner may want the other way.
- (assumption) Assumed a family's cuts are identified from METADATA.pb by weight 700 for bold and style italic for slope, matching how the four shipped families are cut in shipped-face-cuts.ts.
- (question) Does the committed tier gaining cuts breach the offline release asset budget? 31 families at up to four cuts is up to 124 faces against a gate of 90 with about 61 in use. Either the budget rises, or the committed cuts fetch on demand like web faces, or fewer families are committed.
- (question) Does background completion save the document itself, or mark it changed and leave the save to the author? The second is assumed; the first is what 'automatically' could also mean.
- (question) What happens when an upstream family has changed since the document was saved - the Regular embedded in the file no longer matches the Regular upstream? Completing it with today's Bold could pair mismatched vintages of a family.
- (question) Should an author be able to decline completion for a document, and should that decline be remembered, or does every open retry?
- (question) Does the Add font dialog show the author what a family's cuts are before they pick, so a Regular-only family is visible as such at pick time rather than at the moment they press B?
- (question) Does completing a family count as one undoable action or four, and does it appear in the document's undo history at all given the author did not ask for it?

- (decision) The committed tier stays. It is not a cache of fetchable faces - it is a static-instancing pipeline (tools/fontgen/instance_faces.py) that pins the axes of an upstream variable font, because the engine refuses variable fonts by design. These families can exist in this product no other way.
- (constraint) A committed family's cuts are INSTANCED at build time, not fetched. Bold is the same variable source pinned at wght=700, italic comes from the family's italic VF source, and both stay byte-deterministic under SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH with src/out SHA-256 asserted, exactly as the Regulars are. This keeps licence admission on the build gate rather than moving it into the browser.

- (decision) Fresh picks are LAZY too (OWNER): one rule everywhere. Picking a family stores all its cuts in the designer's local face store and embeds only the Regular; each further cut is embedded, and its key added to the chain entry, at the moment the author first uses it. A document that never bolds carries one face, not four.
- (decision) Q6 resolved as FREEZE WHAT IS EMBEDDED (OWNER), superseding the direction to re-save refreshed faces. This keeps the no-op open/save round trip byte-identical, which SPEC-fonts states four separate times.
